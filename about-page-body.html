VT Students First is a volunteer coalition of Vermont parents, alumni, business owners, and neighbors who think the education legislation moving through Montpelier this session is a mistake. There's no outside funding, no paid staff, and no political organization behind this. Just people who live here and have something to lose.

Independent schools like St. Johnsbury Academy, Lyndon Institute, Thetford Academy, and Burr and Burton Academy aren't just schools. For the towns that depend on them, they are part of what makes living here viable. Legislation that doesn't understand that is legislation worth pushing back on.
